# Accessibility

Every component and layout works with a keyboard, a screen reader and reduced motion. Accessibility is part of done, not a later pass. Check new work against WCAG 2.1 AA.

## Color and contrast

- Pair every surface token with its `-foreground` token: `bg-primary` with `text-primary-foreground`, `bg-muted` with `text-muted-foreground`. The pairs are on [Foundations → Colors](?path=/docs/foundations-colors--docs).
- Body text needs a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1, and large text and icons at least 3:1.
- Never use color as the only signal. An error has a message, not just a red border. A status has a label, not just a dot.

## Keyboard and focus

- Everything you can click, you can reach with Tab and use with Enter or Space.
- Focus is always visible. Components show a ring in the `--ring` token on keyboard focus (`focus-visible`). Never remove it without a replacement.
- Focus order follows the reading order. Don't reorder content with CSS in a way that breaks it.
- Dialogs and menus move focus in when they open, keep it inside while open, and return it to the trigger when they close.

## Semantics

- Use the native element for the job: `<button>` for actions, `<a>` for navigation. For a link styled as a button, use `Button` with `asChild`.
- Headings follow the page outline: one `h1`, no skipped levels. See [Guidelines → Typography](?path=/docs/guidelines-typography--docs).
- Every form field has a visible label. Placeholder text isn't a label.
- Errors are linked to their field and announced, not just shown.

<Source
	language='tsx'
	code={`// Navigation keeps a real link
<Button asChild>
	<a href="/projects/new">New project</a>
</Button>`}
/>

## Icons and images

- Icon-only buttons need an `aria-label` that names the action.
- Decorative icons next to text get `aria-hidden="true"`.
- Images that carry meaning have `alt` text. Decorative images have `alt=""`.

<Source
	language='tsx'
	code={`<Button size="icon-md" aria-label="Delete project">
	<Trash2 aria-hidden="true" />
</Button>`}
/>

## Motion

- Respect reduced motion with `useMotionPreference()`. Feedback stays, movement goes.
- Motion is never the only signal of a state change.
- Details are in [Guidelines → Motion](?path=/docs/guidelines-motion--docs).

## Touch and pointer

- Interactive targets are at least 24×24px, and 44×44px where the design allows it.
- Don't rely on hover alone. Anything shown on hover is also reachable with focus or a tap.

## Checking your work

- Tab through the page with no mouse.
- Try it with a screen reader (VoiceOver on macOS: Cmd + F5).
- Turn on reduced motion in your operating system and repeat.
- Zoom to 200% and check that nothing is cut off or overlaps.
